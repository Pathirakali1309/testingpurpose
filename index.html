About us

Two decades of standing beside women who needed a hand up

We are a Chennai-based charitable trust working with women facing poverty, abandonment, domestic violence, and financial hardship. For more than twenty years we have helped women rebuild through education, vocational training, healthcare, counselling, and livelihood support.

When a woman gains independence, her children get a better education, her family becomes financially secure, and her community grows stronger with her.

  • Women-centred development programs
  • Health, nutrition & mental wellness
  • Skill development & livelihood support
  • Education for women & girl children

Our mission & goals

Empower women. Strengthen families. Transform society.

We believe lasting change begins by empowering women with opportunities rather than temporary assistance. Every program focuses on creating confident women who can earn independently, educate their children, and contribute positively to society.

Women's empowerment

Creating self-reliant women through skills, education, and entrepreneurship.

Education

Helping every girl continue her education, regardless of her family's finances.

Community development

Supporting families through awareness programs, healthcare drives, and welfare projects.
