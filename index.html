About us

Fifteen years of turning small loans into small businesses

We are a Chennai-based trust giving interest-free and low-interest microloans to girls and young women who want to start or grow a small business but have no access to formal credit.

A ₹5,000 loan today can become a tailoring shop, a grocery stall, or a home bakery tomorrow — and every rupee repaid funds a loan for the next girl.

  • Collateral-free micro-business loans
  • Financial literacy & budgeting training
  • Business mentorship & market linkage
  • Flexible, low-interest repayment plans

How the fund works

Three simple steps from loan to livelihood

Step 1

Apply & get trained

Girls apply for a loan and complete a short business-readiness and financial literacy workshop before funds are released.

Step 2

Receive the loan

Approved applicants receive a collateral-free micro-loan, typically ₹3,000–₹15,000, along with a mentor matched to their business idea.

Step 3

Repay & grow

Girls repay in small weekly or monthly instalments as their business earns — and repayments fund the next girl's loan.

Our mission & goals

Capital access. Financial confidence. Lasting independence.

We believe girls don't need charity — they need capital and confidence. Every loan comes with training and mentorship so it isn't just money given, but a business built to last.

Access to capital

Collateral-free microloans for girls locked out of formal banking and credit.

Financial literacy

Budgeting, saving, and record-keeping skills that make a loan sustainable.

Mentorship & markets

Pairing each borrower with a mentor and connecting her to buyers and suppliers.
