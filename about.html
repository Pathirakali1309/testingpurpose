About us

Welcome to the fund

Small loans, real businesses, lasting independence

We believe a girl with capital and confidence can build a livelihood no one can take away. Founded on that belief, our trust gives collateral-free microloans to girls and young women who have a business idea but no access to formal credit.

Every loan comes bundled with financial literacy training, a business mentor, and a flexible repayment plan — so a small loan turns into a lasting income, not just a one-time handout.

Every rupee repaid funds a loan for the next girl — turning one loan into hundreds over time.

Mission

Mission statement

Vision statement

"A future where no girl is denied the chance to start a business simply because she has no collateral or credit history."

Leadership

A life dedicated to funding girls' futures

For over fifteen years, our founder has been the guiding force behind this fund — driven by a simple belief: girls don't need charity, they need capital and a fair chance.

Under her leadership, the fund has grown from a handful of small loans into a recognised microloan program reaching hundreds of girls across Tamil Nadu.

Her hands-on approach ensures every rupee lent is tracked, repaid, and relent — so your contribution keeps funding new businesses for years to come.
